feat(26-323): manual booking wizard skips service/worker step when only one option

The first live user (26-321) stalled on a "choose a master" step that offered
exactly one master, not realising she had to tap her own name to select it.
The manual-booking wizard's service and worker steps now each auto-select and
skip themselves when they would offer exactly one option - the service step
when there is exactly one active service, the worker step when exactly one
worker is eligible for the chosen service (the two checks compose, so a
single-service, single-master tenant skips straight from the client step to
the slot step). Several options at either step is unchanged, and so is the
zero-eligible-workers case.

Back-navigation now walks a history stack of steps actually shown, replacing
the old fixed WizardStep -> WizardStep map, since which step is "previous"
now depends on what this particular walk skipped.
